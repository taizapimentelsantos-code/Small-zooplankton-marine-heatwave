# Hobday gap-joining update

Marine heatwaves were recalculated using the conventional event-joining rule: qualifying events separated by gaps of no more than two days were joined (`joinAcrossGaps = TRUE`, `maxGap = 2`). Intervening gap days were included in event duration and monthly MHW-day totals.

## Main changes

| Year | Previous MHW days | Revised MHW days | Previous events | Revised events |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2021 | 74 | 78 | 6 | 4 |
| 2022 | 24 | 26 | 3 | 1 |
| 2023 | 64 | 65 | 8 | 7 |
| 2024 | 147 | 147 | 8 | 8 |

The principal 2024 event was unchanged: 15 February–21 May 2024, 97 days, Category III, maximum intensity 2.05 °C above the climatological mean, and cumulative intensity 116.61 °C-days.

The pre-main-event interval changed from 47 to 48 MHW days. The main-event and post-main-event totals remained 108 and 7 days.

## Inferential consequences

The overall conclusion did not change: no community-level, composition-level, or taxon-level association retained support after Benjamini–Hochberg correction. The seasonally adjusted partial-RDA probability for contemporaneous MHW days changed from 0.0417 to 0.0625, eliminating the previous nominal result below 0.05. Full revised coefficients, probabilities, diagnostics, sensitivity analyses, and adjusted values are provided in the corrected result workbook.
